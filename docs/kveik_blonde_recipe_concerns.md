# Concerns and Analysis — “Kveik Blonde” Recipe Revision

## What Changed Compared to the Previous Version

### Hopstand changes
- Increased Galaxy from 84 g to 100 g
- Increased alpha acids from 13.4% to 15.7%
- Reduced hopstand duration from 30 min to 15 min
- Reduced total calculated bitterness from 21 IBU to 19 IBU

### Overall effect
The beer shifted:
- slightly away from bitterness
- slightly more toward saturated hop aroma
- slightly more modern and juicy

The shorter hopstand is likely a good move for this beer.

---

# Main Concerns

## 1. The beer may still become too thin

The recipe remains:
- 100% base malt
- relatively low OG (1.048)
- highly attenuative yeast
- no dextrin or protein support

Expected finishing gravity:

FG ≈ 1.006–1.008

Potential consequences:
- watery mid-palate
- low body
- weak foam persistence
- “hop sparkling water” impression

## Recommended mitigation

### Option A — Mash higher
Mash at:
- 67–68 °C

instead of lower-temperature highly fermentable mashes.

### Option B — Add body-supporting grain
Possible additions:
- 5–10% wheat malt
- 3–5% chit malt
- 3–5% Carafoam / dextrin malt

The cleanest option is probably:
- 90% pale malt
- 10% wheat malt

---

# 2. Galaxy-only hopping can become harsh

Galaxy is highly expressive, but in very pale and light-bodied beers it can sometimes produce:
- harsh tropical rind character
- green hop edges
- slight onion/dank notes
- rough bitterness perception despite low IBU

The recipe is partially protected because:
- there is no dry hop
- bitterness is moderate
- hopstand temperature is restrained (80 °C)

Still, this remains the biggest flavor-risk variable.

## Recommended mitigation

If the beer tastes too sharp:
- blend Galaxy with a softer hop next time

Good partners:
- Motueka
- Cascade
- Hallertau Blanc
- El Dorado
- Mandarina Bavaria

---

# 3. Hop aroma longevity may be limited

Because:
- there is no dry hop
- there are no late-boil additions
- the beer is light-bodied

…the hop character may peak early and fade relatively quickly.

This may not matter if:
- the beer is consumed fresh
- it is intended as a fast-drinking summer beer

But after several weeks, the beer may become:
- simpler
- more generic
- less expressive

## Recommended mitigation

Drink young:
- ideally within 4–8 weeks

---

# 4. Kveik fermentation temperature is critical

K.22 Stalljen Kveik can become:
- beautifully fruity
or
- overly estery and coarse

depending on fermentation temperature.

## Risks at very high temperatures (35–40 °C)
Possible outcomes:
- fusel alcohol warmth
- overripe orange character
- excessive farmhouse esters
- reduced drinkability

## Recommended range
Suggested fermentation temperature:
- 28–32 °C

This should preserve:
- drinkability
- hop clarity
- balance

while still expressing the Kveik character.

---

# 5. Head retention may be mediocre

The recipe currently lacks:
- wheat
- oats
- higher-protein malt
- dextrin malt

Potential outcomes:
- thin foam cap
- quick foam collapse
- slightly “soda-like” mouthfeel

This is especially relevant because:
- Galaxy oils can suppress foam somewhat
- Kveik can attenuate aggressively

---

# 6. Water chemistry will strongly influence the result

With such a minimal recipe, water becomes very noticeable.

## If sulfate is too high
The beer may become:
- sharp
- dry
- hop-bitter
- mineral-heavy

## If chloride is too low
The beer may feel:
- empty
- thin
- angular

## Recommended direction
A balanced profile leaning slightly chloride-forward would likely work best.

Approximate target:
- Chloride: 80–120 ppm
- Sulfate: 50–90 ppm

---

# 7. The recipe sits between styles

The beer may not fit neatly into:
- classic Blonde Ale
- NEIPA
- Saison
- Farmhouse Ale

Competition judges may struggle with categorization.

However, for casual drinking this is not a problem at all.

In practice, the recipe looks more like:
- a modern hoppy blonde
- a light Kveik pale ale
- a summer craft-lager alternative

---

# Overall Assessment

The revision is probably an improvement over the original version.

The shorter hopstand:
- reduces vegetal extraction risk
- keeps bitterness softer
- should preserve fresher Galaxy aroma

The recipe still looks:
- focused
- restrained
- modern
- highly drinkable

The primary remaining concern is body and texture.

If brewed with:
- proper mash temperature
- good water chemistry
- controlled Kveik fermentation

…the beer could turn out excellent.

Lag ~10h. Free-rose from 24C to 27.1C at peak. Peak 0.00313 SG/h at +19h. 50% of the total drop by +21h, 90% by +37h. Flat at ~1.006 from +45h until the crash at ~+78h, which gave a ~30h warm hold after terminal.

**Highest attenuation of any pale Verdant batch (89%).** Most likely because it's a raw ale: without a full boil, mash enzymes are not completely denatured and keep converting dextrins in the fermenter. A 65°C mash and a Pilsner base also help.

The Pill's cold plateau (1.004) is 2 points below the hydrometer FG (1.006), which matches the warm plateau instead. Usually the cold Pill reading agrees best with the hydrometer, so this one is unusual — the hydrometer value is used.

Grist: 71% Golden Promise, 10% oat malt, 10% wheat, 4.3% flaked oats, 4.3% Carabody. Mash 67°C / 60 min. Single hop: Centennial.

| Day | Date | SG | Temp | Drop |

| 4 | 2026-10-11 | 1.017 | 1.3C | cold crash |
| 5 | 2026-10-12 | 1.017 | 0.8C | stable |

*Readings between +45h and +70h are noisy (a single 1.0085 reading at +48h, then 1.014–1.018 swings), consistent with a dry-hop addition or other fermenter disturbance. The script's daily value of 1.0091 for day 2 is that artifact and was replaced with the clean hourly median.*

Lag ~7h (first 2-point drop). The ferment free-rose from 26C to **29.9C** at peak activity, giving the **fastest peak rate (0.00394 SG/h at +16h) and fastest completion (~+33h) in the Verdant dataset**. 50% of the total drop was complete by +16h and 90% by +26h. Gravity sat flat at ~1.019 from +31h to +42h.

**Likely dry-hop creep:** the pre-dry-hop plateau was ~1.019 and the cold plateau after crashing was 1.017 (hydrometer FG 1.018). Cold readings normally come out 0.5–1.6 points *higher* than warm ones on this Pill, so a ~2 point *lower* cold reading points to a small amount of real post-dry-hop fermentation.

Attenuation of 71% is below the Verdant average, as expected from the 67°C mash, 4.3% Carabody and ~24% oat/wheat in the grist. Cold crash started at ~+73h.

## Statistical Analysis

### Peak Fermentation Timing

| Stat | Hours after pitch |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 7.5h (#6, warm pitch 24C, low OG) |
| Maximum | 25.2h (#78, cooler pitch 22C, higher OG) |
| Mean | **19.2h** |
| Median | **19.0h** |

*n=9 (updated with #85 and #96).*

**Key factor: ferment temperature.** Warm ferments that free-rise above 28C (#8, #65, #96) peak at 16-20h; ferments held at 20-23C peak at 24-25h.

### Fermentation Completion

| Stat | Hours after pitch |
|---|---|
| Minimum | **~33h (#96, free-rise to 30C)** |
| Maximum | ~240h (#22, braggot with honey sugars) |
| Mean (excl. braggot) | **56h** |
| Median (excl. braggot) | **52.5h** |

*n=8 excluding the braggot (updated with #85 and #96).*

Standard all-grain beers finish in **1.5-3 days**. A warm free-rising ferment (#96) can be done in under 1.5 days. Beers with adjuncts (honey, lactose) take longer.

### Peak Fermentation Rate

| Stat | SG/hour |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 0.00124 (#22, low-gravity braggot) |
| Maximum | **0.00394 (#96, free-rise to 30C)** |
| Mean | **0.00293** |

*n=9 (updated with #85 and #96).*

### Apparent Attenuation

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 61% (#81, Marmalade Stout — specialty malts) |
| Maximum | 89% (#22 braggot — honey; #85 raw ale — enzymes not denatured) |
| Mean (pale all-grain, excl. braggot & stout) | **79%** |
| Median (pale all-grain, excl. braggot & stout) | **78%** |
| Mean (pale, boiled wort only — also excl. raw #85) | **77%** |

*n=7 pale all-grain batches (updated with #85 and #96).*

Lallemand spec says 77% attenuation. Our pale all-grain results average **77%**, right on spec. The spread (71-83%) tracks the grist and mash: #96 (67°C mash, Carabody, ~24% oat/wheat) and #78 sit at 71-72%, while simpler grists reach 78-83%. The raw ale #85 reached 89% because enzymes survived the 1-minute boil.

## Estimations for Future Brews

Based on this data, for a typical NEIPA/hazy (OG 1.050-1.070) with Verdant IPA at 20-24C:

| What | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Lag phase (no visible activity) | 6-12h |
| Peak fermentation rate | 18-25h after pitch (16-20h if free-rising above 28C) |
| 50% of total gravity drop | By ~24h (~16h if warm) |
| 90% of total gravity drop | By ~48h (~26h if warm) |
| Terminal gravity reached | 49-72h (~33h if warm) |
| Expected attenuation | 76-83% (71-72% with a 67°C mash and heavy oat/wheat/dextrin grist; up to ~89% for raw / no-boil ales) |
| Safe to dry hop (if within 5 pts of FG) | ~36-48h (~24-30h if warm) |
| Safe to cold crash | ~72h |
| Expected FG from OG | OG - (OG - 1.000) * 0.77 (boiled wort); × 0.89 for raw ales |

**Dry-hop creep:** expect FG to drop a further 1-2 points after dry hopping (seen on #96). Leave a day or two after the dry hop before packaging so it finishes in the fermenter.

### Batch #82 Hazy Motueka Blonde — Final Results

- OG: 1.052, pitched Mar 18 ~12:00 UTC at ~27C (cooled to 22C working temp)
- FG: **1.012** (77% attenuation — in line with Verdant average)
- Peak at **+25h** (0.00226 SG/h) — on the slower end, consistent with 12h lag phase and 22C working temp
- Terminal gravity at **+49h** — faster than the 56h median, likely aided by warm pitch start
- Spunding valve closed at SG 1.021 (+33h, 15 PSI) → ~2.4 vol CO₂
- Cold crashed at +80h. After 25h at 0C, beer remained fully opaque (high protein grain bill: 43% oat/wheat adjuncts)

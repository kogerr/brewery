## Water

| Ion | Target (ppm) |
|---|---|
| Ca | 60 |
| Mg | 5 |
| Na | 10 |
| Cl | 100 |
| SO4 | 80 |
| HCO3 | 0 (RO base) |

Cl:SO4 ratio ~1.25:1 — slightly chloride-forward to support the fruity/soft character without full NEIPA levels. Mash pH 5.35.

## Process

1. Mash at 64°C for 60 min (dry, fermentable wort — lets fruit character shine)
2. Boil 30 min with Idaho 7 at start
3. Hopstand: cool to 80°C, add El Dorado + Nectaron, hold 15 min
4. Chill to 33°C, pitch Eitrheim
5. Ferment at 33–35°C — expect ~72h to terminal
6. Dry hop at terminal gravity (or 1–2 points above) — add all three dry hop additions together
7. Leave dry hops 3 days, then cold crash
8. Package — spunding at 12–15 PSI during fermentation for natural carbonation, or force carb to 2.4 vol

## Expected Character

- **Aroma:** Apricot, peach, nectarine, ripe stone fruit, with a dank/resinous undercurrent from Idaho 7
- **Flavor:** Juicy stone fruit, clean bitterness with slight resin, dry finish
- **Mouthfeel:** Medium body from oats/wheat, dry finish from low mash temp, carbonation lifts the fruit
- **The Idaho 7 dank edge** prevents it from being one-dimensional — gives it IPA structure and prevents "fruit smoothie" territory

## Notes

- The oats + wheat provide body and haze stability without needing a high FG
- If the beer is too dry/thin, raise mash to 65–66°C next time
- If Idaho 7's dankness dominates, reduce dry hop to 20g and increase El Dorado to 60g
- Nectaron is expensive/hard to find — Galaxy (peach + passionfruit) is a workable substitute
- K.14 Eitrheim is not on Hopline — source from Kveik Yeastery directly

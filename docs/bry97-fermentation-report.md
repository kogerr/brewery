## Temperature Influence

| Pitch Temp Range | Batches | Avg Peak Timing | Avg Peak Rate | Avg Attenuation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20-23C | #37, #57 | 37.7h | 0.00225 | 78% |
| 24-26C | #61, #60, #53, #33, #26, #7 | 23.6h | 0.00227 | 79% |
| 27-30C | #77, #55, #50, #46, #44, #39, #19 | 24.5h | 0.00247 | 79% |

### Key temperature observations:

1. **Cooler pitches (20-23C) delay the peak** by ~12h compared to warmer pitches but don't significantly affect final attenuation.

2. **Very warm pitches (28-30C) risk stress and stalling** — #46 (Lupomax Black IPA, 29C) peaked fastest (13.7h) but stalled at 70% attenuation. #39 (29C spike on day 1) also stalled at 69%.

3. **24-26C is the sweet spot** — consistent peak timing around 24h, reliable attenuation around 79-80%.

4. **Summer brews run hot** — batches #46, #44 both saw temps rise to 30C during active fermentation. Both had fast peaks but #46 under-attenuated.

## BRY-97 vs Verdant IPA Comparison

| Metric | BRY-97 (n=15) | Verdant IPA (n=7) |
|---|---|---|
| Peak timing (mean) | **25.9h** | 19.6h |
| Peak timing (median) | **25.2h** | 18.9h |
| Peak rate (mean) | 0.00239 | 0.00276 |
| Completion (median) | **72h** | 56h |
| Attenuation (mean) | **79.2%** | 79% |
| Attenuation (median) | **80.6%** | 78% |
| Typical temp range | 22-26C | 22-25C |

**BRY-97 peaks about 6 hours later** than Verdant on average and takes about a day longer to finish. Peak rates are slightly lower. However, final attenuation is essentially identical — both land around 79-80%.

BRY-97 is more tolerant of temperature variation (rated 15-22C but commonly pitched at 25-28C in this dataset) while Verdant tends to be used in a tighter window.

The biggest difference is fermentation profile: Verdant drops fast and finishes quickly (often 48-56h), while BRY-97 has a more gradual curve with a longer tail — the classic "slow and steady" dry yeast profile.

## Data Quality Notes

- **RAPT Pill SG readings during cold crash are unreliable.** Several batches show apparent SG drops or spikes when temperature drops below 5C. This is a known issue — the pill's accelerometer is affected by density changes from temperature, and CO2 coming out of solution during cold crash further distorts readings.
- **"Done" detection algorithm** can be confused by cold crash stability. The "Done (h)" values in the summary table were manually estimated from daily gravity data rather than using the raw algorithm output.
- **#64 Blonde IPA** had extreme sensor anomalies (SG readings of 0.87 during cold crash) — fermentation data excluded from statistics.
- **#2 White Double IPA** had the pill apparently stuck/dislodged — SG plateaued at 1.025 for 2 weeks despite measured FG of 1.009. Peak and done timings excluded.
